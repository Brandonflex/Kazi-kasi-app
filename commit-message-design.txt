Major design system overhaul: Editorial craft + brand identity

Implements comprehensive visual redesign based on user-provided design assets:
- Template (dark editorial marketing page)
- Two brand identity systems (GLM 5 Turbo + Mercury 3)

Design System v2:
- Typography: Fraunces (display, editorial serif) + Inter (body, modern sans)
- Palette: Espresso (#14110d) + Bone (#f7f2e8) + Ochre (#f0ac3e) + Terracotta (#c96f4a)
- Motion: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) ease-out, 350ms transitions
- Shape: 24px radius cards, 999px pill buttons, warm layered shadows
- Glass-morphism header with backdrop-filter blur
- Scroll-triggered reveal animations with staggered delays

Brand Identity (synthesized from Mercury 3 + template):
- "K" monogram logo in rounded square (espresso on bone)
- Editorial masthead pattern: kicker → display heading → scopeline
- Warm, organic, human — anti-AI aesthetic (no neon, no glow)

Component Updates:
- Job cards: Editorial styling, hover lift, border-color transitions
- Buttons: Pill-shaped, ochre primary, tactile hover states
- Chips: Warm bone background, ochre hover, smooth transitions
- Header: Sticky, glass-morphism, scroll-aware background
- Feed header: Editorial kicker + Fraunces display heading

Visual Language:
- Warm bone background (not stark white)
- Ochre accent for CTAs and highlights
- Terracotta secondary for warnings/offers
- Sage green for success states
- Espresso for primary text and dark elements

This is the foundation for the full application redesign. Next phases will
apply this design language to the tracker, shortlist, and other pages.

Assets saved to docs/assets/ for reference.
