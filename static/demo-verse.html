✨ HDR Bloom Effects

Every job orb glows with post-processing bloom, creating a cinematic, immersive atmosphere. High match scores pulse brighter.

What you'll see: Job orbs with golden glow that pulses based on match score. Higher matches = brighter glow.

🌌 5000-Particle Starfield

A vast cosmic background of twinkling particles creates depth and immersion. The universe feels alive.

What you'll see: Thousands of tiny stars in the background, slowly twinkling and drifting.

🚀 Warp Drive Animation

Click any job to warp the camera to it with a smooth 2-second animation. Feel like you're traveling through space.

What you'll see: Camera smoothly warps to selected job with streaking light effects.

🗺️ Interactive Minimap

Top-right corner shows an orthographic view of the entire universe. Click to jump to any region.

What you'll see: Mini overview with camera position indicator. Click anywhere to navigate.

🎵 Spatial Audio

Ambient drone creates atmosphere. Hover over jobs for subtle ping sounds. Warp drives have swoosh effects.

What you'll hear: Low-frequency ambient hum, high-pitched hover pings, warp swoosh sounds.

Toggle audio with the mute button in the top-right corner.

🎮 Full Interactivity

  • Drag to rotate the universe
  • Scroll to zoom in/out
  • Right-click drag to pan
  • Click job orbs to warp to them
  • Search to highlight matching jobs
  • Filter by industry (tech, finance, healthcare, education)
  • Autopilot mode tours top matches automatically

Keyboard shortcuts: M (minimap), A (autopilot), R (reset view), F (future feed)
