The guardrails

Fifteen hard
anti-patterns.

Not suggestions. Not roadmaps. Hard rules the product refuses to ship.

No crypto-only job boardsFintech-flavored hustle ≠ hiring.
No AI ghost listingsEvery opening must be real and owned by a human.
No password-first authWhatsApp is the login.
No English-only copySwahili, English, Sheng — all first-class.
No salary-less listingsTransparency is a feature.
No infinite scrollCalm feeds. Clear intent.
No recruiter white-labelingRecruiters use the real thing or none of it.
No vague “exciting opportunity” copySpecific beats thrilling.
No pre-2020 designModern craft, local warmth.
No dark-pattern pricingFree for job seekers, period.
No county-blind matchingProximity is the point.
No email-only updatesWhatsApp, SMS, voice notes.
