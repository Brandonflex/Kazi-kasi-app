# Brand identity

**In one line:** *Kazi-Kasi is the scope, not the catalogue — it tells you which
jobs you can actually reach and take, and proves it.*

---

## 1. The name

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kazi** | *work, job, occupation* — Swahili, class IX noun; the everyday word on every job advert in Kenya |
| **Kasi** | *speed, velocity, force* — Swahili (from Arabic *qāsin*); "kwa kasi" = quickly |
| **Kazi-Kasi** | **work — and fast.** The hyphen is the pause between the two, not a compound noun |

The reading we are choosing is deliberately plain: **work, quickly.** Not
"career acceleration", not "talent marketplace". A person should be able to hear
it in a matatu and understand it.

Reduplication is common in Swahili for emphasis, which is why the doubled sound
lands as *"kazi, properly"* rather than as a product code-name. We are not
inventing a word; we are putting two that everybody already knows next to each
other, and owning the pairing.

### The naming risk, stated plainly

"Kazi" alone is not ours to own:

- **KaziChat, KaziKit, KaziNest, SwiftKazi** already trade on it.
- **BrighterMonday's** LinkedIn tagline is *"Kazi Kwako!"* — the word is in a
  competitor's mouth, in the same market.
- It is a generic Swahili noun, which makes it weak as a trademark on its own.

Three consequences for the identity:

1. **The mark must carry the brand without the word.** The Scope Mark (§4) is
   legible at 16 px with no lettering.
2. **The hyphen is part of the identity.** *Kazi-Kasi*, never "KaziKasi" or
   "Kazi Kasi", so the pairing is what is searched for and what is remembered.
3. **Trademark strategy is a KIPI class 35 + 42 filing on the wordmark**, with
   the device mark as the fallback if the word is refused. Domain and handle
   checklist already lives in `docs/research/06-brand-ip-check.md`.

## 2. Positioning

> For early-career Kenyans who are applying into a market where 200–500 people
> chase every entry-level advert, **Kazi-Kasi is the job scope that only shows
> what you can actually reach, take and act on today** — because it verifies
> freshness, eligibility, distance and risk before it shows you anything, and
> then says which of those it checked.

**What we are:** a scope. A short, explained list. A daily nudge on the phone
you already have.

**What we are not:** a job board (we do not host applications), an "AI career
coach", a CV builder, a marketplace of employers, a social network for
professionals, or a place that gamifies your job search.

The category word we use internally and in copy is **"job scope"**, not "job
board". If a sentence would work on BrighterMonday's homepage, it is the wrong
sentence.

## 3. Voice

**Who is speaking:** a cousin who has worked in Nairobi for a few years, knows
which adverts are real, and is not impressed by job titles.

| We do | We do not |
|---|---|
| "3 km away — walkable, approximate." | "Located in the heart of Westlands!" |
| "closes in 5 days" | "Hurry, don't miss out!" |
| "Pay not stated — most adverts don't say." | "Competitive salary" |
| "This one is flagged: asks for a fee." | "⚠️ SCAM ALERT ⚠️" |
| "Nothing to send today." (silence, in the digest) | "No jobs found. Try again tomorrow!" |
| "We check this board every 15 minutes. It is 24 minutes late." | "Real-time updates!" |

**Seven rules**

1. **No exclamation marks.** Ever. Urgency is a date, not a tone.
2. **Numbers beat adjectives.** A distance, a deadline, a count, a timestamp.
3. **Say what we don't know.** "Eligibility unstated" is a feature; the advert
   is silent, and pretending otherwise is how people waste a morning applying.
4. **Name the uncertainty.** "approximate", "not re-checked recently" — hedging
   that is specific reads as competence; hedging that is vague reads as evasion.
5. **Never blame the applicant.** "Nothing matches these constraints yet —
   loosen one" is a product statement; "you're not qualified" is not ours to say.
6. **Swahili only where it is already universal.** *kazi*, *safari*, *pole
   pole*, *matatu*, *harambee*. No machine-translated Swahili, ever — a
   half-fluent sentence costs more trust than an English one.
7. **Second person, active voice.** "You applied on 14 Oct", not "application
   recorded".

### Copy specimens

```
Digest subject line (WhatsApp):  *Kazi-Kasi* — 5 jobs for you today
Card reason:                     matches admin · closes in 5 days
Empty state:                     Nothing to send today.
Blocked card:                    Can't take this one: needs 3 years, you have 0.
Risk:                            Flagged: asks for a fee before interview.

Footer:                          Reply STOP to stop these. Reply HELP for the web feed.
```

## 4. The Scope Mark

The mark is **three concentric arcs, cropped by a horizon line** — a radius
being measured from a point, which is the entire product: *you, your reach, the
work inside it.*

```
        ╱‾‾‾‾‾╲          three arcs = the commute bands the product already uses
      ╱  ╱‾‾‾╲  ╲        (walkable / one matatu / long commute)
     │  │  •  │  │       the dot = you
      ╲  ╲___╱  ╱
        ╲_____╱          cropped at the bottom = the horizon, the edge of the map
     ─────────────
```

**Construction rules**

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Grid | 24 × 24, 2 px live area inset |
| Stroke | 2 px, round caps, **never** fewer than 1.5 px at 16 px |
| Arcs | evenly spaced, 1:1 gap-to-stroke |
| Dot | 2.5 px radius, centred on the arcs' origin, sitting on the horizon |
| Monochrome only | Ink on paper, paper on forest. No gradient, no multi-colour |
| Minimum size | 16 px (favicon, WhatsApp profile avatar 640 × 640 export) |
| Clear space | 25% of the mark's height on all sides |

**Why not a briefcase, a location pin or a "K":** all three are already on
Kenyan job adverts, and the pin specifically means *a place*, while the product
means *a reachable radius from a person*. The scope is the one shape that says
"this is measured from where you are".

**Lockups**

- **Primary:** Scope Mark + *Kazi-Kasi* wordmark, mark left, 8 px gap, hyphen
  retained.
- **Stacked:** mark above the wordmark, for the WhatsApp profile and app icon.
- **Mark only:** favicon, message avatar, loading state.
- **Wordmark only:** never in a space narrower than 96 px.

**Wordmark note:** set in the system UI font at 700 weight, letter-spacing
−0.02em, sentence case (*Kazi-Kasi*, not *KAZI-KASI*). Meta's display-name rules
reject all-caps and symbols, so the WhatsApp business name must be exactly
`Kazi-Kasi`.

## 5. Colour

Four colours, one job each. The palette is warm-paper and ink with a forest
structural colour and an amber urgency colour — **no blue**, because blue is the
default of every bank and every generic SaaS, and we are neither.

| Token | Hex | Role | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#12161C` | All body text, the mark | Black is one of the Kenyan flag's four and reads as *the people*; at 17:1 on paper it is also simply legible in sunlight |
| **Paper** | `#FBF8F2` | Page background | Warm neutral, not grey: equatorial light is strong and warm, and cool greys look dirty next to it |
| **Forest** | `#14532D` | Primary action, "verified/live", links | Growth and stability, and Kenya's green. **Deliberately darker than Safaricom's green** so it reads as institution, not telco |
| **Amber** | `#9A4E00` | Deadlines, "closing soon", attention | Warm ochre is the confident East African accent; used only for time pressure, never for brand decoration |
| **Risk red** | `#A3241C` | Scam flags, blocked jobs, errors — **nothing else** | If red ever means "act now", it stops meaning "be careful" |

Rules:

- **One accent per card.** A card is either announcing a deadline (amber) or a
  verification (forest) or a risk (red). Never two.
- **Red is never a call to action.** Buttons are forest; primary actions are
  never red.
- **No colour-only meaning.** Every badge carries a word: *live*, *closes in 5
  days*, *flagged*.
- **Contrast is a build gate.** Every pair in [01](./01-visual-system.md) passes
  WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large), and `tests/test_design.py` computes the
  ratios from the shipped CSS so a future palette tweak cannot quietly fail it.

## 6. Typography

**System stack only.** No webfonts. The reasons are practical, not aesthetic:

- On a 2–4 GB Android phone on metered data, a 100–300 KB font file is a
  measurable delay for zero comprehension gain.
- Roboto/Noto on Android already render Swahili diacritics and Latin Extended
  correctly.
- The site must work at 320 px and at 200% zoom without a layout dance.

```
--font-sans: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif;
--font-mono: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, monospace;   /* the digest preview, numbers in tables */
```

| Role | Size / line-height | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | 28 / 1.15 | 700, −0.02em | Page title only |
| Title | 18 / 1.3 | 650 | Job title on a card |
| Body | 16 / 1.55 | 400 | Everything readable |
| Small | 13 / 1.45 | 400 | Ages, timestamps, captions |
| Label | 12 / 1.2 | 600, +0.04em | Badges, uppercase sparingly |

**Numerals:** `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` wherever numbers are compared
(pay, distances, deadlines) so columns do not jitter.

## 7. Motion and imagery

- **Motion:** one transition, 120 ms ease-out on hover/focus. No entrance
  animations, no parallax. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables even that.
- **Loading:** the only allowed animation is the scope sweep (a 700 ms arc
  rotate), and only if a spinner is needed at all.
- **Photography:** none in v1. Stock photos of smiling graduates are the visual
  language of the thing we are replacing, and they cost data.
- **Illustration:** none. The evidence *is* the visual: timestamps, sources,
  badges, distance bands.

## 8. WhatsApp as the primary brand surface

Most people will meet the brand in a chat list. That constrains the identity
more than the website does:

| Constraint | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Display name approved by Meta, must match external branding | exactly `Kazi-Kasi`, no emoji, no slogan |
| Green tick requires Business Verification + 30 days + 2FA | plan for unverified launch; trust comes from the copy, not the badge |
| Text-only until templates are approved | the identity lives in **structure**: bold title, one fact line, one reason, one link |
| 4096-character limit, 160-character SMS fallback | the brand must survive being cut to one segment |
| No branding inside the bubble | no logos, no colour; the voice and the ordering *are* the brand |

That last row is the important one. In WhatsApp we control: the order of the
cards, the words, and the one link. That is the whole design surface, and it is
why the digest is written like a telegram rather than a newsletter.

**Profile assets to produce (not yet made):** 640 × 640 mark on paper, an "about"
line under 139 characters (`Jobs you can reach, checked today. Reply STOP to
stop.`), and a business description that repeats the scope promise.

## 9. Governance

- **No new colour without a role.** Adding a sixth hue means writing down what it
  means and where red/amber stop meaning it.
- **Tokens live in `serve.py` (`STYLE`) today** and are asserted by
  `tests/test_design.py`; when a component library exists, that test moves with
  the tokens.
- **The mark is versioned** (`/docs/design/assets/` when assets exist). Any change
  to the arcs is a brand change, not a tweak.
