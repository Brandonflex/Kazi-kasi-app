## 3. Design Direction

### Human-Centered, Anti-AI Aesthetic

**Core Principles:**
- **Authenticity over polish** — Hand-crafted feel, intentional imperfection
- **Warmth over sterility** — Earthy palettes, organic shapes, human touch
- **Clarity over cleverness** — Clear hierarchy, predictable patterns
- **Motion with purpose** — Feedback and guidance, not decoration

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Deep forest green (#0d4d2b) — trust, growth, stability
- Secondary: Warm clay (#c97b5a) — earthy, human, approachable
- Accent: Muted gold (#d4a574) — warmth, quality, craft
- Background: Soft cream (#faf8f5) — warm, readable, natural
- Text: Rich charcoal (#2c2c2c) — softer than pure black

**Typography:**
- Display: Custom serif with human imperfection (not geometric sans)
- Body: Warm, readable sans-serif with open letterforms
- Accent: Handwritten/variable font for human touch

**Visual Elements:**
- Organic blob shapes for backgrounds and dividers
- Subtle film grain texture overlay
- Hand-drawn icons and illustrations
- Purposeful asymmetry in layouts
- Soft shadows and light skeuomorphism

## 4. Animation & 3D Concepts

### Purposeful, Not Decorative

**Micro-interactions:**
- Button press with tactile feedback (scale + shadow)
- Form validation with gentle color transitions
- Job card hover with subtle lift and shadow
- Loading states with organic motion (not spinner)

**Page Transitions:**
- Smooth fade + slide (not abrupt cuts)
- Staggered entrance animations for lists
- Parallax depth on scroll (subtle, not overwhelming)

**3D Elements:**
- Job cards with subtle depth and layering
- Profile section with spatial hierarchy
- Interactive map for job locations (Kenya focus)

**Motion Principles:**
- Fast (150-300ms), responsive to user action
- Natural easing (ease-out, not linear)
- Respect reduced-motion preferences
- Guide attention, don't distract

## 5. Language & Copy Guidelines

### Human Voice, No Robotic AI Speak

**DO:**
- Write like a person talking to a friend
- Use contractions (it's, we're, you'll)
- Be specific and concrete (not vague)
- Show personality and warmth
- Use Kenyan context and references

**DON'T:**
- Use "leverage," "utilize," "seamless," "robust"
- Write like a corporate brochure
- Sound like AI-generated content
- Use generic phrases ("cutting-edge," "world-class")
- Be overly formal or stiff

**Examples:**

❌ "Leverage our seamless platform to access robust job listings"
✅ "Find real jobs from companies that are actually hiring"

❌ "Utilize advanced filters for optimal job discovery"
✅ "Filter by salary, location, or deadline — find what fits your life"

❌ "Experience world-class career opportunities"
✅ "Get jobs that match your skills and your schedule"

## 10. Anti-AI Design Checklist

**Visual:**
- [ ] Hand-drawn or custom illustrations (not stock AI art)
- [ ] Organic shapes, not perfect geometry
- [ ] Subtle texture (film grain, paper)
- [ ] Imperfect alignment (intentional)
- [ ] Warm, earthy color palette

**Copy:**
- [ ] Conversational tone
- [ ] Specific examples (not vague promises)
- [ ] Kenyan context and references
- [ ] Personality and humor
- [ ] No corporate jargon

**Interaction:**
- [ ] Responsive to user action
- [ ] Forgiving of mistakes
- [ ] Clear feedback
- [ ] Predictable patterns
- [ ] Respect for user's time
